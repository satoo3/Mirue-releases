# Mirue Privacy Policy

[日本語](PRIVACY.md)

Mirue ("the App") respects your privacy. This page explains what information the App handles, and how.

## Information we collect

The developer of the App does not collect any personal information. There is no account registration and no
server run by the developer. The App uses no advertising and no analytics.

## Information handled only on your Mac

- **Images you open**: The App reads and shows the images you open on this Mac only. It does not change them or
  send them anywhere. Only when you choose "Move to Trash" does it move that image to the Trash.
- **File names in the same folder**: To move to the previous or next image, the App reads the names of the files
  in the folder of the image you opened.
- **Settings**: Your display settings, and a record of the apps that opened images before you switched the
  default app, are saved on this Mac (`~/Library/Preferences/jp.ultrawarp.mirue.plist`).
- **Recent items**: Images you open are recorded in macOS's Recent Items. This is a macOS feature and stays on
  your Mac.
- **Clipboard**: Only when you choose "Copy" does the App put the image you are viewing on the clipboard.

## Network

The App does not communicate over the network.

## Downloads

The App is distributed on GitHub. GitHub's
[General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
applies to the download itself.
